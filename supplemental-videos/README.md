# Supplementary videos

These videos accompany the manuscript **Label-free volumetric dynamic atlas of fresh mouse organs using dynamic full-field optical coherence tomography**.

These are the original MP4 files supplied with the manuscript; no resizing or re-encoding was applied for this GitHub copy.

| Visualization | Video file | Description | Corresponding figure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Visualization 1 | [VideoS1-Whole Brain of Mouse.mp4](VideoS1-Whole%20Brain%20of%20Mouse.mp4) | Large-area D-FFOCT imaging of freshly excised mouse brain in the coronal plane. | Fig. 2(a) |
| Visualization 2 | [VideoS2-Mouse_Brain_3D.mp4](VideoS2-Mouse_Brain_3D.mp4) | Three-dimensional D-FFOCT reconstruction of a hippocampal region in fresh mouse brain. | Fig. 2(d) |
| Visualization 3 | [VideoS3-Mouse_Eyeball_3D.mp4](VideoS3-Mouse_Eyeball_3D.mp4) | Three-dimensional D-FFOCT reconstruction of a corneal region in a fresh mouse eyeball. | Fig. 3(d) |
| Visualization 4 | [VideoS4-Mouse_Liver_3D.mp4](VideoS4-Mouse_Liver_3D.mp4) | Three-dimensional D-FFOCT reconstruction of a selected region in fresh mouse liver tissue. | Fig. 6(d) |
| Visualization 5 | [VideoS5-Mouse_Brain_30h_timelapse.mp4](VideoS5-Mouse_Brain_30h_timelapse.mp4) | Time-lapse D-FFOCT imaging of dynamic contrast in freshly excised mouse dentate gyrus tissue over 30 h. | Fig. 13(a)–13(f) |
| Visualization 6 | [VideoS6-Mouse_Liver_20h_timelapse.mp4](VideoS6-Mouse_Liver_20h_timelapse.mp4) | Time-lapse D-FFOCT imaging of dynamic contrast in freshly excised mouse liver tissue over 20 h. | Fig. 14(a)–14(f) |
